## What I do as a PM

### Product Strategy → Shippable Scope
- Define problems worth solving and tie them to outcomes (time saved, revenue lift, adoption, error reduction)
- Turn ambiguity into clear requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and edge-case handling
- Break big initiatives into small releases that can ship and be measured

### UX-Driven Product Development
- Design flows that remove friction and reduce training burden
- Build “guardrails by default” so users do the right thing without thinking
- Partner closely with design and engineering to keep the product coherent and usable

### Execution & Delivery
- Own backlog hygiene, prioritization, sprint planning, and release coordination
- Align stakeholders around tradeoffs (scope, timing, risk), then deliver
- Instrument what matters and iterate based on usage and results

---

## How I build products
- Ship small, learn fast, iterate hard
- Prefer clarity over cleverness (especially in requirements)
- Make the “right way” the easiest way
- Measure what matters: adoption, time-to-value, error rate, and downstream operational impact
